How this was built

The stations begin on the 5th rather than the 1st, so the wheel sits deliberately out of step with both the calendar months and the zodiac. Nothing here is inherited. The names describe what light and weather are doing across the year in the northern hemisphere, and each stone was chosen for a real physical property that echoes it — obsidian for the Veil because it is volcanic glass that takes an edge, petrified wood for the Amber Hour because it is a tree that became quartz.

It is a made-up system, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise. Read it the way you would read a good horoscope: as a frame that makes you look at something more carefully than you otherwise would.
