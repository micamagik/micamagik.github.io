The Stone Index

✦ Mineral names · families · hardness · plain English ✦

A quick reference for stones Mica Magik covers. The index describes materials; it does not turn folklore into geology.

18 stones

✦ TYPE TO FILTER THE INDEX ✦

Quartz

Silicate · Mohs 7

Silicon dioxide; the mineral behind clear quartz and many familiar varieties.

Copper

Native element · Mohs 2.5–3

A native element valued for colour, conductivity, and workability.

Shungite

Carbon-rich mineraloid · Mohs Variable

A carbon-rich material associated with Karelia, Russia.

Herkimer Diamond

Quartz / locality term · Mohs 7

Double-terminated quartz associated with Herkimer County, New York.

Opal

Hydrated silica · Mohs 5.5–6.5

A hydrated silica mineraloid with variable water content.

Agate

Microcrystalline quartz · Mohs 6.5–7

A banded variety of chalcedony and quartz.

Tourmaline

Borosilicate · Mohs 7–7.5

A mineral group with complex borosilicate chemistry.

Fluorite

Halide · Mohs 4

Calcium fluoride, often found in vivid colours.

Moonstone

Feldspar gem material · Mohs 6–6.5

A feldspar gem material showing adularescence.

Amethyst

Quartz variety · Mohs 7

Purple quartz; much colour is associated with iron and irradiation.

Pyrite

Sulfide · Mohs 6–6.5

Iron sulfide with metallic lustre and brassy colour.

Petrified Wood

Silicified fossil wood · Mohs Variable

Fossil wood altered or infilled by minerals.

Citrine

Quartz variety · Mohs 7

Yellow to orange quartz; much commercial material is heat-treated.

Calcite

Carbonate · Mohs 3

Calcium carbonate and a relatively soft mineral.

Selenite

Gypsum variety · Mohs 2

A clear or translucent variety of gypsum.

Labradorite

Feldspar · Mohs 6–6.5

A feldspar known for labradorescence.

Obsidian

Volcanic glass · Mohs 5–5.5

Natural volcanic glass rather than a crystalline mineral.

Tiger's Eye

Quartz aggregate · Mohs 6.5–7

A quartz-rich material with chatoyancy.
